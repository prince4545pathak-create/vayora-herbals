01 / Brand story

About VAYORA HERBALS

VAYORA HERBALS is a fictional brand developed for an educational marketing project. The concept explores how a herbal-inspired knee-care gel could be positioned for adults who want to maintain comfort during everyday movement.

🌿

Brand idea

A nature-inspired personal-care concept built around simplicity, thoughtful presentation and responsible communication.

🎯

Main objective

To present a product concept focused on supporting everyday comfort and an active lifestyle, without promising to diagnose, treat or cure a medical condition.

👥

Target audience

Adults aged 50+ and families exploring convenient external-use personal-care products. The concept is designed with accessibility and clear labelling in mind.

07 / Compliance & operations

Licensing, quality and safety

Important: VAYORA is a fictional school-project concept, not a licensed or approved medicine. No licence, registration, clinical testing or manufacturing approval is claimed on this website.

Before any real product could be manufactured, advertised or sold, its intended classification and applicable Indian requirements would need to be determined by qualified regulatory professionals and the relevant authorities. Depending on classification, requirements may include appropriate manufacturing permissions, product approvals, quality controls, compliant labelling, ingredient review, safety assessment and advertising compliance.

Any real formulation would require qualified formulation work, testing, documented specifications, suitable manufacturing facilities, batch records, stability checks and appropriate packaging controls. No home-made formulation or medical use is recommended.
